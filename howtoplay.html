How to Play?

  1. 1 Get ready to climb the Tower of Wit and begin your adventure!
  2. 2 Choose your preferred topic: History, Food, or Places.
  3. 3 Select the weather to determine your difficulty mode.
  4. 4 Choose the animal partner that matches your selected weather.
  5. 5 Answer the multiple-choice questions before the timer runs out.
  6. 6 Be careful with your answers because you have limited hearts!
  7. 7 Earn points by answering questions correctly and continue climbing.
  8. 8 Complete all five levels and reach the top of the Tower of Wit!
